How Can I Help

There are two simple ways to help the library grow: give books, or give some of your time.

Give Books

The shelves are not full, and this only grows if other people join in. If you have books to give, these are the ones that will actually get read:

  • Hindi picture books and stories for children under twelve
  • Hindi non-fiction for young readers — science, biography, history, the natural world
  • Fiction and classics in Hindi, and in English for stronger readers
  • Books about this part of the country — its history, its people, its rivers and forests
  • Anything you loved as a child and think a child here would too

Please don't send textbooks, exam guides, question banks, damaged books, or English picture books for very young children. A book nobody here can read — or wants to — occupies a shelf that a better book should be on. If you're unsure, write first.

Volunteer

If you are in or around Chopan and would like to help, there are many ways to contribute. You can help us organise and catalogue books, spend time reading with children, help visitors find books, assist with library activities, or help us build and maintain the collection.

You don't need to be a librarian or have any particular qualification. What matters is that you are willing to give some time, be dependable, and help make the library a welcoming place for people to read and learn.

As the library grows, there will be more ways to get involved, from documenting local history and culture to helping build resources about Sonbhadra.

If you are in or near Chopan, the most useful thing you can give is two hours. Read with the children, teach something on a Sunday, or simply keep the door open on a fixed day.

If you'd like to volunteer, get in touch with us.
