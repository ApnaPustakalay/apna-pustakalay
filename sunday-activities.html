Sunday Activities

अपना पुस्तकालय

Puzzles, games and conversations that wander well beyond the usual reading.

What happens

On Sundays, the library turns into something a little different — less about picking a book off the shelf, and more about doing things together.

Some weeks it's a jigsaw puzzle of the map of India, working out where the states and cities actually are. Other weeks it's a conversation around a globe, or a board game, or whatever the kids happen to be curious about that day.

There's no fixed syllabus for these sessions, and no sign-up required. Any child who wants to join is welcome to sit down with everyone else.

If you'd like to help run a Sunday activity, or have an idea for one, get in touch.
