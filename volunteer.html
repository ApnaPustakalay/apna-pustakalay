Volunteer with Us

A library needs people as much as it needs books.

What you can do

If you are in or around Chopan and would like to help, there are many ways to contribute. You can organise and catalogue books, read with children, help visitors find books, assist with library activities, or help build and maintain the collection.

You don't need to be a librarian or have any particular qualification. What matters is that you are willing to give some time, be dependable, and help make the library a welcoming place to read and learn.

As the library grows, there will be other ways to get involved, including documenting local history and culture and helping build resources.

Before you sign up, ask yourself

  • Can I commit to a regular day or time, at least once every couple of weeks?
  • Am I comfortable spending time with children and reading with them?
  • Can I get to Chopan, or am I already close by?
  • Will I show up when I say I will, and let someone know if I can't?
  • Am I doing this because I want to, not because it looks good on paper?

Even two hours every couple of weeks can help. You might read with children, teach something on a Sunday, help organise the books, or help us keep the library more inclusive.

If you'd like to volunteer, get in touch with us.
