Volunteer

A library needs people as much as it needs books.

If you are in or around Chopan and would like to help, there are many ways to contribute. You can help us organise and catalogue books, spend time reading with children, help visitors find books, assist with library activities, or help us build and maintain the collection.

You don't need to be a librarian or have any particular qualification. What matters is that you are willing to give some time, be dependable, and help make the library a welcoming place for people to read and learn.

As the library grows, there will be more ways to get involved, from documenting local history and culture to helping build resources about Sonbhadra.

If you'd like to volunteer, get in touch with us.
